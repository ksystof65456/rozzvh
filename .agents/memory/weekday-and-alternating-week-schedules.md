---
name: Weekday and alternating-week schedules
description: Product rules for school days, retained weekend data, and fortnightly lessons.
---

The timetable should show Monday through Friday only; the user says school does not take place on weekends in their area. Preserve any historical Saturday/Sunday records when hiding weekend days rather than deleting user data. Alternating-week classes begin on a selected Monday and recur every two weeks from that anchor.

**Why:** These are explicit product expectations, and weekend entries may still be existing user data.

**How to apply:** Keep new schedule entry limited to weekdays. For biweekly lessons, use the selected current/next Monday as the recurrence anchor and calculate visibility from that date.
